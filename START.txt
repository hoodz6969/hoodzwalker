HoodzWalker
3D modelling for GTA V and FiveM

1. Unzip this folder anywhere except Program Files.
2. Run hoodzwalker.exe. Nothing else to install.
3. In the app: GTA > Game files browser, and point it at your GTA V folder (the one with GTA5.exe).

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, DirectX 11.
This zip does not include GTA V. You need your own copy of the game.
